# Meaning Supremacy System (MSS)

A formal axiomatic framework proposing that the universe's fundamental substrate is discrete logical structure, not continuous spacetime. Observable physics emerges as low-resolution projections of this underlying logical field.

## Core Claims

1. **Meaning Ontology (A1)**: Existence = logical self-consistency. What "exists" is what can sustain logical coherence under the irreducible cost of computation.

2. **Thermal Tax (A3)**: Every logical operation pays an irreducible cost — the Thermal Tax. This is not metaphor; it is the MSS analog of thermodynamic entropy, quantifiable and testable.

3. **Information Slicing (A2)**: Perception = projection of the meaning field through an observer's perceptual shell. Different observers see different "reality" because they sample different projections.

4. **Discrete Foundation**: Continuity is an emergent approximation at large scales (low resolution), not a fundamental property. The Planck scale is the logical pixel size.
