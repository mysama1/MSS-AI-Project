# MSS-AI: Potential First Clients (10 Targets)

## Selection Criteria
- Pain point matches MSS-AI strengths (consistent reasoning, compliance, low cost)
- Budget exists (willing to pay for better results)
- Decision maker accessible (not through procurement bureaucracy)
- Problem is knowledge-intensive (not just data processing)

## Tier 1: Immediate (0-4 weeks)

### 1. Pharmaceutical R&D - Drug Interaction Analysis
**Pain**: Drug-drug interaction prediction has high false positive rate
**MSS-AI value**: Discrete logic graph reduces hallucination risk
**Contact**: CRO project managers at Shanghai Zhangjiang
**Approach**: Offer free POC with public drug interaction dataset

### 2. Small Law Firm - Contract Review Assistant
**Pain**: Junior lawyers spend 60% time on routine clause checking
**MSS-AI value**: Rule-based consistency, no probability drift
**Contact**: Boutique law firms in Shenzhen/Hangzhou
**Approach**: Monthly subscription, 9999 yuan/year basic

### 3. Manufacturing QA - Defect Root Cause Analysis
**Pain**: Complex manufacturing defects hard to trace to root cause
**MSS-AI value**: Causal graph reasoning, explainable outputs
**Contact**: Factory managers in Suzhou/Dongguan
**Approach**: POC with 3-month free trial, then per-line pricing

### 4. Banking Compliance - Regulation Change Monitoring
**Pain**: New regulations require manual review of thousands of internal documents
**MSS-AI value**: Zero-hallucination compliance checking
**Contact**: Compliance officers at city-level commercial banks
**Approach**: 29999 yuan/year industry edition

### 5. Medical Records - Diagnosis Consistency Check
**Pain**: Junior doctors miss rare disease indicators in patient records
**MSS-AI value**: Differential diagnosis reasoning chain
**Contact**: Tier-2 city hospital department heads
**Approach**: Clinical decision support, NOT replacement
